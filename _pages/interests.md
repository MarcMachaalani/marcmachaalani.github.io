# Beyond medicine

I studied piano with Loucine Panjarian at the Lebanese National Higher Conservatory of Music from 2006 to 2021. Some of my music is on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@MarcMachaalani), though most of what I’ve posted there isn’t piano. I listen to all sorts of music, especially classical music and jazz. Oscar Peterson is my favorite jazz pianist.

When I have an afternoon free, I like to play tennis, go for a bike ride, or spend a little time in nature around New Haven. I also enjoy a game of chess. You can find me on [Lichess](https://lichess.org/@/Marcotitto) as Marcotitto.

My bookshelves lean toward classics and history. I keep track of what I’m reading on [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/marcmachaalani). I love classic movies, too, and keep a film diary on [Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/Marcotitto/).
